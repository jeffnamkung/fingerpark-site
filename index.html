Build parks. Skate the world.

Fingerpark is a skatepark builder and fingerboard skating game for iPhone. Design a park block by block or describe it in words, let four AI pros score it, then skate it yourself and roll out onto real streets anywhere on Earth.

Contact

Email jeffnamkung@gmail.com with questions, bug reports or ideas. Include your iPhone model and iOS version, and a park share link if the problem is in a particular park.

Common questions

How do I steer?
In the Skate tab, slide a finger slowly sideways on the deck to turn. Swipe down beside the board to push, and hold beside it to brake. Flick up from the tail to ollie; the tricks list is on the Skate tab. Prefer a thumbstick? Switch to “Stick + board”.
A real place won't load, or looks empty.
Maps come from OpenStreetMap over the internet, so check your connection and tap Retry. Streets and buildings load first; extra detail such as benches and street lamps can take longer or appear on a later visit. Places OpenStreetMap hasn't mapped yet will look sparse.
How does “Describe a park” work?
Open the Build tab and tap Describe. It uses your own Anthropic API key (create one at console.anthropic.com); each design usually costs a few cents on your Anthropic account. Describe the park, then ask for changes until it's right. Undo takes back any design.
How do I share a park?
Use the Share tab to send a link or code. Anyone with Fingerpark can open it.
Can I play my own music?
Yes. Fingerpark mixes with Spotify, YouTube Music and other apps, and can play from your Apple Music library.
